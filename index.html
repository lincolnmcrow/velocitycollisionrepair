Questions Answered

COMMON FAQ.

Here are a few common questions to help you know what to expect before reaching out.

What repairs do you offer?

Collision repair, body work, paint and refinishing, and other exterior repair needs. Contact the shop to discuss your specific vehicle.

Do you work with insurance?

We can help you understand the repair process when insurance is involved. Coverage, approval, and payment depend on your policy and carrier.

Can I request an estimate?

Yes. Use the inquiry form to share your contact details, vehicle information, and a description of the damage.

Is my vehicle drivable?

If you are unsure, avoid driving a vehicle that may be unsafe. Contact the shop and explain the condition so next steps can be discussed.

How long will repairs take?

Repair time depends on the damage, parts availability, and the work required. A specific timeline can be discussed after the vehicle is assessed.

Do I need an appointment?

Use the inquiry form or contact the shop to confirm availability and the best way to arrange an estimate.
